# The Crowded Table

Shared language for community membership, audiences and activities.

## Communities and audiences

**Community account**:
A person's account within one specific community. Having a community account does not by itself mean the person is in good standing.

**Good standing**:
Having a community account whose standing is approved or suspended. Suspension retains this audience classification while limiting particular actions.

**Community member in good standing**:
A person with approved or suspended standing in the community being viewed.

**Non-member**:
A viewer outside the community's good-standing audience, including a signed-out visitor, a person without a community account, or a person with pending or inactive standing. A non-member may still have a community account and separately authorized access to their own records.

**Community-public**:
Accessible to any community member in good standing, subject to the resource's lifecycle and applicable action rules. This does not mean accessible to non-members or to the anonymous web.

## Activities

**Official activity**:
An event or table officially sponsored by the community.

**Unofficial activity**:
An event or table hosted by a community member without official community sponsorship.

**Open activity**:
An activity whose admission rules permit authenticated non-members to participate, subject to its other eligibility requirements. Open describes admission, independently of official sponsorship or anonymous visibility.
_Avoid_: Public activity (when referring to non-member admission)
